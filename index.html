Amerix's 5 Non-Negotiable Health Principles

Master these biological fundamentals to eliminate chronic fatigue, optimize hormones, and unfat your body.

🔥

1. Fasting & Autophagy

Give your digestive system complete rest. Fasting triggers autophagy—cellular cleanup where your body scavenges and recycles damaged, senescent cells and excess visceral fat.

🚫

2. Zero Seed Oils & Sugars

Eliminate canola, vegetable, corn, and sunflower oils. Cook exclusively with grass-fed tallow, butter, or ghee. Refined sugar is a metabolic toxin that destroys liver function.

☀️

3. Morning Sun & Early Sleep (9:30 PM)

Expose your eyes and skin to natural morning sunlight within 30 minutes of waking. Be in bed by 9:30 PM in total darkness to maximize nocturnal human growth hormone release.

🏋️

4. Heavy Resistance Training & 10,000 Steps

Squat, deadlift, and press heavy weights 3 times a week. Accumulate 10,000 daily steps. Dense skeletal muscle is your premier metabolic sink and longevity shield.

💧

5. Saline Hydration & Cold Showers

Plain distilled water flushes electrolytes out of your cells. Add natural rock or sea salt to your water. Take cold showers daily to sharpen dopaminergic resilience and activate metabolism.
